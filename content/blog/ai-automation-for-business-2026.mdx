## How Small Businesses are Using Custom AI Workflows

The real magic happens when you move beyond basic chatbots and start building **Custom AI Workflows**. This is where OpenClaw shines. OpenClaw allows you to connect your specific business logic, your preferred software (CRMs, scheduling tools, billing systems), and powerful LLMs into a seamless, automated process.

Here are three real-world examples of how local businesses are thriving with AI automation in 2026:

### 1. The 24/7 Dental Receptionist

A dental practice in Chicago implemented an OpenClaw AI agent on their website and phone system.
*   **The Workflow:** When a patient calls or messages after hours, the AI agent accesses the practice's scheduling software. It asks triage questions ("Are you in pain?", "Is this a routine cleaning?"), verifies insurance information using an API, and books the appointment directly into the calendar.
*   **The Result:** A 40% increase in new patient bookings, simply by capturing leads that previously went to voicemail.

### 2. The Automated Roofing Estimator

A regional roofing company struggled with the time-consuming process of providing initial quotes.
*   **The Workflow:** They built an AI workflow where a potential customer uploads a photo of their house and answers three simple questions via a Telegram bot (hosted effortlessly on ClickRunClaw). The AI analyzes the photo, calculates approximate square footage based on public real estate data, and instantly texts back a preliminary estimate range and a link to book an in-person inspection.
*   **The Result:** Sales reps now only visit highly qualified leads who are already comfortable with the price range, doubling their close rate.

### 3. The Digital Agency Lead Qualifier

A boutique digital agency was overwhelmed by unqualified leads draining their time.
*   **The Workflow:** They set up a custom OpenClaw agent that engages with every inbound lead via email. The agent asks qualifying questions about budget, timeline, and project scope. If the lead meets the criteria, the AI agent automatically creates a project in their CRM, drafts a personalized proposal template, and alerts the sales director.
*   **The Result:** The agency founders reclaimed 15 hours a week previously spent on discovery calls with bad-fit clients.
